Proof Chain

A proof-of-work chain where the puzzle is an open Erdős problem. Each block is a Lean statement. Provers race to submit a proof, or a disproof, and the Lean kernel checks it. The first proof that passes mints the block, and the credit goes to whoever wrote it.

This is a simulation. The ten problems are real and were open as far as I know; check erdosproblems.com for current status. Every submission, proof and result here is simulated, and decades of work are compressed into seconds. The Lean statements are simplified sketches. Real formalizations of many Erdős problems live in Google DeepMind's Formal Conjectures library.

    The puzzle
    Each block is an open problem stated in Lean. Its statement hash is locked into the block, so nobody can quietly prove an easier version.
    
    
    The work
    Provers, human or AI, search for a proof. Almost everything they submit fails. Finding a proof is the hard part.
    
    
    The check
    Verification is cheap and exact. The kernel either accepts the proof or it doesn't. No sorry, no extra axioms, same statement. Unlike image models, there's no noise.
    
    
    The catch
    Nobody can schedule a breakthrough. Real Erdős problems may take decades per block, so a working chain would need easier puzzles in between, such as lemmas or known results awaiting formalization.
